The "Doctrinal Vulnerability & Ratio Extraction" Prompt (Google AI Studio)

Attach your full-text judgment or Parliamentary Bill PDF into Google AI Studio (Gemini 1.5 Pro / Flash) with 2M token context.

Act as a Senior Research Fellow at the National Law University, Delhi.
I have attached the full judgment / bill text in [Case / Bill Name].

Perform a strict doctrinal analysis:
1. State the exact Ratio Decidendi formulated by the majority. Cite the specific paragraph numbers.
2. Isolate the strongest vulnerability, minority dissent, or concurring caveat noted in the opinion.
3. Identify any latent friction between this ruling and existing statutory provisions (e.g., LARR Act 2013, RERA 2016, or Trade Marks Act 1999).
4. Propose 3 sharply defined, argumentative thesis statements for a 1,200-word law review blog post that advances a reformative critique rather than a descriptive case summary.

Draft a 1,250-word analytical law review blog submission titled: "[Direct, Academic Title]".

Required Structure:
I. The Factual & Statutory Impasse (Max 200 words - strictly analytical, no long narrative history).
II. The Doctrinal Fault-Line (500 words - critique the legal fallacy, statutory contradiction, or economic inefficiency).
III. Practical Ramifications for Practitioners & Affected Stakeholders (350 words - evaluate real-world impact).

Style & Guardrails:
- Objective, persuasive, and dense legal prose.
